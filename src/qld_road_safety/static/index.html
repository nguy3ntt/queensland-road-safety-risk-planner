How to read this view

Expected weight is the model's forecast of future serious-crash burden for one quarter. The interval shows uncertainty. Abstained segments are withheld from the shortlist and blue map overlay, but remain in coverage counts and the overview layer. The ranking is for investigation, not an instruction to treat a road.
