Queensland Government source data

Source data are attributed to the Queensland Government departments below under CC BY 4.0. This project transformed source geometry and traffic records, map-matched and allocated crash events, and produced historical model scores. This acknowledgement does not imply government endorsement.

  • Department of Transport and Main Roads: crash data, road location and traffic data, and traffic census; snapshots retrieved 25 September 2026.
  • Department of Natural Resources and Mines, Manufacturing and Regional and Rural Development: Queensland Roads and Tracks; complete state-controlled snapshot captured 26 September 2026.

Road geometry is a 2026 snapshot displayed retrospectively. Forecasts are held-out historical demonstrations, not current predictions or treatment effects.
